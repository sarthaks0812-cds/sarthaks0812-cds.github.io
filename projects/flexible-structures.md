## Modelling and identification

The model accounts for rigid-body motion and elastic deformation using Hamilton’s variational principle. The beam discretization uses Galerkin’s method and Hermite elements.

## Experimental work

Estimated elastic and damping properties from vibration data measured using laser displacement sensors and IMUs. FFT-based natural-frequency estimation supported model validation.

## Outcome

Natural frequencies predicted within ±0.1% error; modal participation factors estimated from experimental data.
