## Mechanical subsystem

Led mechanical design for Team Tiburon. Designed a modular frame and arranged eight BlueRobotics T100 thrusters to increase manoeuvrability from four to six degrees of freedom.

## Structural validation

Used lightweight modular aluminium extrusions and performed hydrostatic structural analysis to check hull integrity at depths of 0.5–1.0 m.

## Outcome

Reduced vehicle mass from 21 kg to 20.28 kg.
