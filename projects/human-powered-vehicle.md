## Chassis and ergonomics

Designed a semi-recumbent two-wheeler chassis. Studied rider posture, vision and joint kinematics in CATIA using fifth- and fiftieth-percentile manikins.

## Analysis and simulation

Performed static and modal analyses of the rollover protection structure in ANSYS. Simulated a landing-gear mechanism in Simscape Multibody and computed traction parameters and battery sizing in MATLAB/Simulink.

## Outcome

Reduced structural deformation from 0.10 mm to 0.07 mm; simulated controlled landing-gear deployment within 8 seconds.
