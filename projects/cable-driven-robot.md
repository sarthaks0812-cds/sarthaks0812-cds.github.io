## Design and actuation

Optimized the workspace and actuator layout of an underactuated cable-driven parallel robot. Used closed-loop stepper motors, Dyneema cables, custom 3D-printed winch drums and flexible couplings.

## Control and validation

Developed LabVIEW FPGA pulse-frequency-modulated signals for synchronized position control of three actuators. Tested step, ramp and sinusoidal motor responses using incremental encoder feedback and Arduino/Python data acquisition.

## Outcome

Achieved ±5 mm trajectory-tracking repeatability.
