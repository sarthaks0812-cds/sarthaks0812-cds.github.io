## Mechanical design

Designed and prototyped a wheeled dual-arm mobile social robot, considering user height and stability. Integrated wheel motors for omnidirectional movement and arm motors for two-degree-of-freedom manipulation through UART.

## Modelling

Derived arm kinematics geometrically and dynamics using the Euler–Lagrange method. Modelled and validated the arm in MATLAB Simscape Multibody.

## Outcome

Contributed to Indian Patent No. 579221, granted January 29, 2025.
